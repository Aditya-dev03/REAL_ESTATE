## 3. Data Scope & Important Real Estate Disclaimers
- **Portal Listings vs. Registered Transactions**: 
  The records in this dataset represent **online real estate portal listings and seller asking prices**, rather than legally registered deed sales or municipal stamp-duty transfer deeds. 
  *Notice*: In Indian real estate transactions, final negotiated closing prices commonly diverge from initial online asking prices due to builder discounts, negotiation margins, stamp duty / registration overheads, and floor-rise or parking surcharges.
- **Geographic Scope**: Metropolitan Bengaluru (Bangalore), Karnataka, India.
- **Currency & Units**: 
  Target price is denominated in **Lakhs of Indian Rupees (INR)**.
  - 1 Lakh INR = ₹100,000 (0.1 Million INR).
  - 100 Lakhs INR = ₹1 Crore (10 Million INR).
  - Living and parcel areas are predominantly denominated in **Square Feet (sq. ft.)**, with a minority of listings recorded in square meters, acres, gunthas, perches, and grounds.
